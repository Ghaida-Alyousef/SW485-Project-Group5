Original Purpose:
According to the Kaggle description, the dataset provides airline
booking information for analyzing demand, popular routes, and
customer preferences. It includes booking characteristics,
optional service preferences, and a booking completion indicator.

Purpose in This Project:
The dataset will support the CCO Flight Booking & Passenger
Advisory Dashboard. The team will use supervised classification
to predict booking completion and examine relevant booking
patterns to inform commercial recommendations.

Data Handling:
customer_booking.csv is retained as the original downloaded file.
Preprocessing will be documented separately, and cleaned data
will be saved as preprocessed_data.csv.
